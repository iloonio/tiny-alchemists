# Hiiiii Designer team!!!

In order to play the game: 
1. you will have to select the *QuickPlaytest* scenario before pressing play.
2. Press play Afterwards, Look to the *Hierarchy* and find the *NetworkManager* inside of the *DontDestroyOnLoad* section. 
3. Look for the button: "Start as Host". 
4. Press the "Start as Host" button, and voilà! You are in the game :D
